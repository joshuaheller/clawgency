What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an open source framework for autonomous AI agentswho can complete complex tasks independently. Originally developed under the codenames Clawdbot and Moltbot, OpenClaw enables companies to create AI agents that access their own data and systems - from email replying to full process automation.

OpenClaw & enterprise security

In contrast to closed SaaS solutions, OpenClaw offers full data control. Companies in the global market can operate agents on their own servers (on-premise) or in a privacy-compliant hybrid cloud. Clawgency ensures that all OpenClaw deployments comply with strict US and international compliance requirements.

Why OpenClaw instead of alternatives?

OpenClaw combines the Flexibility of open source with the power of state-of-the-art AI models. Compared to ChatGPT Enterprise, you retain data sovereignty. Compared to no-code tools like n8n or Zapier, you get true AI agent capabilities with custom skills, API integrations and autonomous decision making.

OpenClaw is a Open source framework for autonomous AI agents, which was originally developed under the codenames Clawdbot and Moltbot. It enables companies to create AI agents that independently handle complex tasks - from answering customer queries to data processing to full process automation. Unlike simple chatbots, OpenClaw agents can access corporate data, make decisions and perform actions in connected systems.

OpenClaw offers three key advantages: full data control (on-premise or in your own cloud), real agent skills (autonomous decision making, not just simple workflows like n8n/Zapier) and no vendor lock-in (Open source, you keep the code and control). For companies in the global market that value privacy compliance and data sovereignty, OpenClaw is the ideal choice.
